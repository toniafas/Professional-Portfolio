Introduction
This project explores the evolution of Artificial Intelligence, from its early theoretical foundations to modern applications of machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI. It provides an overview of significant milestones that have shaped the development of intelligent technologies.
Read the full artifact details
Description
A visual timeline highlighting important developments in AI, including early foundational research, expert systems, AI winters, neural networks, deep learning, and recent developments in generative AI.

Artifact 02 · Applied AI portfolio

AI Lab Portfolio: From AI Experimentation to a Working Business Prototype

Introduction
A portfolio of applied AI, prompt engineering, research evaluation, design thinking, and chatbot development, completed for Machine Learning Fundamentals (AIML-500). It uses SCRUBIES, my handwoven tote bag business, as the practical case.
Read the full artifact details
Description
Five connected activities: a generative AI experiment comparing three ChatGPT runs of one structured prompt; iterative prompting practice with a GPT; a research-assistant review using STORM AI; problem-solving with Design Thinking; and building and testing the SCRUBIES AI Shopping Assistant in Chatbase.
Objective
To evaluate generative AI critically and explore how AI can improve customer experience and business performance in small e-commerce businesses, moving from experimentation to a working prototype.
Process
I compared three ChatGPT outputs for the same Context-Task-Constraints prompt, practiced six interaction skills in an iterative conversation, reviewed a STORM AI research article and its brainstorming process, applied the five stages of Design Thinking to SCRUBIES, then built the chatbot in Chatbase and tested it across seven customer scenarios.
Tools and Technologies Used
ChatGPT
STORM AI (Stanford Open Virtual Assistant Lab)
Chatbase
Design Thinking and prompt engineering
Value Proposition
Unique Value. Shows how I move from testing AI outputs to questioning them and protecting customer trust. Testing showed that a fluent chatbot can still repeat inaccurate information, so accurate data and human oversight remain essential.

Relevance. Demonstrates prompt engineering, critical evaluation of AI output, research interpretation, user-centered design, chatbot prototyping and testing, and responsible AI practice: accuracy, privacy, transparency, and human oversight.

Artifact 03 · AI communication

Neural Networks: Communication for Learning

Introduction
This artifact demonstrates how I used generative AI and iterative prompt engineering to explain neural networks in a simple, relatable way. I used my handwoven tote bag business, SCRUBIES, as a practical example to connect machine learning concepts to everyday business applications.
Read the full artifact details
Description
The project explores how neural networks learn patterns from data through their input, hidden, and output layers. Using tote bag images as an example, I demonstrated how a computer could learn to recognize different colors, patterns, and designs.
Objective
To demonstrate an understanding of neural networks and explore how effective prompting, simplified explanations, and visual communication can make technical concepts more accessible to nontechnical audiences.
Process
Initial Prompt: Asked ChatGPT to explain neural networks in machine learning.
Prompt Refinement: Requested a simpler explanation using clear language and fewer technical terms.
Visual Development: Requested a labeled visual illustrating the input, hidden, and output layers.
Business Application: Applied the concept to SCRUBIES to demonstrate how neural networks could recognize and categorize tote bag designs.
Final Review: Refined the explanation to improve clarity, relevance, and audience understanding.
Tools and Technologies Used
ChatGPT – AI-generated explanations and prompt refinement.
AI Image Generation – Creation of an educational visual.
Prompt Engineering – Iterative prompting and output refinement.
Value Proposition
Unique Value. This artifact demonstrates my ability to translate technical AI concepts into relatable business examples. It also highlights my ability to use AI tools thoughtfully to create accessible and engaging explanations.

Relevance. The skills demonstrated are relevant to machine learning, business analytics, and AI adoption, where understanding technology and communicating its value are essential.

Artifact 04 · Responsible AI & strategic leadership

Navigating Human Bias in AI-Driven Business Decisions

Introduction
AI can help organizations make faster, more informed decisions, but its recommendations are only as reliable as the data and assumptions behind them. This artifact examines how human cognitive biases can influence AI systems and explores practical leadership strategies for promoting fairness, accountability, and responsible AI adoption.

Using examples from revenue operations and business intelligence, I considered how historical business practices can shape predictive models and how leaders can prevent technology from reinforcing existing blind spots.
Read the full artifact details
Objective
To explore the relationship between human bias, AI-driven decision-making, and organizational change, while developing practical strategies for responsible AI integration in business environments.
Process
I began by examining cognitive biases, including confirmation bias, survivorship bias, and automation bias, and considering how they influence business decisions and AI systems.

I then applied these concepts to a realistic business scenario involving predictive lead scoring, where historical sales practices could unintentionally influence which customers receive attention.

Using generative AI as a collaborative learning tool, I developed and refined a personal leadership value statement and five practical strategies addressing data quality, diverse perspectives, human oversight, employee engagement, and psychological safety.

I reviewed the work for clarity, professional relevance, and alignment with responsible AI and change leadership principles.
Tools and Methods
ChatGPT – brainstorming, iterative refinement, and critical review
Cognitive bias analysis
Responsible AI and fairness principles
Change management and leadership frameworks
Business intelligence and predictive analytics scenarios
Value Proposition
Unique Value. This artifact demonstrates my ability to connect AI concepts with business strategy and ethical leadership. It highlights my approach to identifying risks in data-driven decisions, questioning assumptions, and developing practical strategies that balance technological innovation with human judgment.

It also reflects my understanding that successful AI adoption requires more than technical implementation. Organizations need transparent decision-making, employee involvement, continuous evaluation, and leaders who remain accountable for the outcomes.

Relevance. Responsible AI adoption, business analytics, strategic decision-making, risk awareness, cross-functional collaboration, and organizational change leadership.
